Markera scores a precision target from a photo. Point the camera at the target and tap Mark: the app finds the holes, reads the ring numbers, fits the centre and the rings to the target's own lines, and scores every hit, inner tens as X. Correct a score with one tap, add a hit the detection missed right on the photo, or move and remove hits with your finger.

Every series is saved on your phone with its photo, time, calibre and tag. No account needed. Sign in with Google or Apple to keep your series on your account and see them on another phone too.

Import photos
Score photos you already have: pick many images at once from the gallery or your files. Auto import saves every image that scores and shows you the rest afterwards. The date the photo was taken becomes the time of the series.

Insights
Markera reads your hits per calibre and tells you what it sees, with advice on what to try: a group off centre, a stretched group, fliers, a wide group, a weak series or day, a trend over the weeks, and whether you get better or worse during a session. When your group sits off centre day after day, the sight helper says how far to move the rear sight, in millimetres, and in clicks once you enter your sight's click value. The strongest new finding shows up on the home screen.

Statistics
Pick calibre, tag and period and see every hit on a drawn target, coloured from older to newer, with group size, spread, mean and median point of impact and mean score. Trend plots any measure over time, per calibre if you like. Drift shows how your point of impact moves from day to day.
