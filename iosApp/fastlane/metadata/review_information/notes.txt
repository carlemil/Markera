Scanning works without an account: on the scan screen, tap the photo icon in the corner of the viewfinder to pick a saved photo of a precision target, or aim the camera at one, then tap "Detektera". Sign in with Apple is only needed to save series and see history and statistics; any Apple ID works, there is no separate demo account. Saved series and the account can be deleted from inside the app ("Radera konto" on the home screen).
