Markera scores a precision target from a photo. Point the camera at the target and tap Mark: the app finds the holes, reads the ring numbers, fits the center and the rings to the target's own lines, and scores every hit, inner tens as X. Correct a score with one tap, add a hit the detection missed right on the photo, or move and remove hits with your finger.

Every series is saved on your iPhone with its photo, time, caliber and tag. No account needed. Sign in with Apple to keep your series on your account and see them on another iPhone too.

Import photos
Score photos you already have: pick many images at once from your photo library or Files. Auto import saves every image that scores and shows you the rest afterwards. The date the photo was taken becomes the time of the series.

Insights
Markera reads your hits per caliber and tells you what it sees, with advice on what to try: a group off center, a stretched group, fliers, a wide group, a weak series or day, a trend over the weeks, and whether you get better or worse during a session. When your group sits off center day after day, the sight helper says how far to move the rear sight, in millimeters, and in clicks once you enter your sight's click value. The strongest new finding shows up on the home screen.

Statistics
Pick caliber, tag and period and see every hit on a drawn target, colored from older to newer, with group size, spread, mean and median point of impact and mean score. Trend plots any measure over time, per caliber if you like. Drift shows how your point of impact moves from day to day.
